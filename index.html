Healthcare System

Three-Tier Care Architecture

Understanding how primary optometry, independent surgical providers, and NHS Hospital Eye Services (HES) interact.

Tier 1

Primary Optometry

High-street optometry practices (Boots, Specsavers, Independents) managing primary sight tests, GOS, and initial pathology detection.

Tier 2 · EyePros

Independent Sector Provider

Consultant-led independent clinics delivering fast-track self-pay and NHS community-commissioned cataract, glaucoma, and laser eye procedures.

Tier 3

NHS Hospital Eye Services (HES)

Acute NHS hospital trusts handling high-complex tertiary pathology, emergency trauma, and long-term complex ocular disease backlogs.

Intraocular Pressure

Fluid pressure inside the eye measured in mmHg. Normal range is 10-21 mmHg. Primary metric in glaucoma diagnosis and MIGS surgical evaluation.

Clinical

OCT

Optical Coherence Tomography

Flip card
Optical Coherence Tomography

Non-invasive cross-sectional retinal imaging. Essential for macular disease, dry AMD drusen tracking, and optic nerve fiber layer analysis.

Surgical

MIGS

Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery

Flip card
Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery

Micro-stent implantation to lower IOP with minimal tissue disruption. Ahmad's subspecialty edge over traditional trabeculectomy.

NHS body responsible for regional healthcare commissioning. Nottingham & Nottinghamshire ICB holds the public sector contract keys.
