7b. Commercial Operations

KPI Scorecard 30% Work / 70% Results

Work Metrics (30%)

  • First visits made: 18/week
  • Follow-ups done when due: 9 out of 10
  • Visits written up same day: everyone
  • Good conversation criteria met: 4 out of every 6

Results Metrics (70%)

  • Practices that sign agreement: 4/month rising to 6
  • Of those, how many send a patient: 6 out of 10
  • Referrals arriving: more each month
  • Practices that stop sending: fewer than 2/month

30/60/90 Day Milestones

First 30 Days

  • ~70 first visits done
  • 4 in 6 visits reach decision-maker
  • At least 4 practices agree to refer
  • All 28 group owners found, 8 contacted
  • First referrals arriving

First 60 Days

  • ~140 visits done
  • Second meetings happening
  • At least 10 practices agreed total
  • At least 4 group meetings held
  • Referrals going up month by month

First 90 Days

  • ~210 visits done
  • Queue of booked meetings
  • At least 16 practices agreed total
  • At least 2 groups agreed
  • Three months of records to review

Friday Report Framework

  • Block 1 — Work this week: visits, good first visits, conversations, follow-ups, referrals
  • Block 2 — Pipeline: how many practices at each of the 6 stages, what moved
  • Block 3 — Problems: anything getting in the way (3 lines enough)
  • Block 4 — Next week: 3 things to focus on, no more

12. Final Recommendations

  1. Prioritize Digital Acquisition: Front-load the £50K marketing budget into high-intent Google Ads to drive early self-pay volume while PMI pathways mature.
  2. Aggressive Optometrist Outreach: The £865K pipeline requires immediate action. Dedicate one day per week purely to visiting local practices.
  3. Premium Lens Conversion: Train staff deeply on communicating the value of toric and multifocal lenses to increase revenue per patient safely.
  4. Review Management: Implement an automated system to capture Google Reviews post-discharge to build social proof rapidly against established chains.
  5. MIGS Differentiation: Heavily feature advanced glaucoma surgical options in B2B marketing, as this is a clear differentiator from standard cataract factories.
