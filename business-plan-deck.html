Who We Are

Executive summary and founding clinical vision

October 2026

EyePros Overview (ELSAHN LTD · Companies House 07909477)

EyePros is a high-growth private ophthalmology practice led by two consultant ophthalmic surgeons (Mr Ahmad Elsahn & Ms Yasmine Abdalla).

• FY2026 Actual Revenue: £230,309 at 94.9% gross margin on current outpatient caseload.
• Strategic Objective: Bring surgery in-house at Kingfisher House, Nottingham — eliminating third-party hospital list fees and saving ~£1,575 per eye vs The Park Hospital.
• Practice Philosophy: Uncompromising named-consultant continuity across all 7 core subspecialties in an unhurried, boutique clinical environment.

Our Vision

To become the premier consultant-led ophthalmic clinic group in the Midlands and the UK, defining the gold standard for clinical excellence, transparent pricing, and subspecialty depth.

Our Mission

Deliver unhurried, named-surgeon continuity from initial consultation through surgery to discharge. Combine all 7 ophthalmic disciplines under one roof with transparent pricing and compassionate, boutique care. “See What's Possible.”

Red Ocean vs Blue Ocean

Strategic positioning: making the corporate volume competition irrelevant

October 2026

RED OCEAN · Volume Factory Market

• 10-min appointment slots, 30+ eyes per day assembly line.
• Rotating locum surgeons — different doctor every consultation.
• 1–2 specialties only (cataract OR high-street laser, never both).
• Race to the bottom competing on low-margin NHS contracts.
• Monofocal-only — no premium lens upsell or lifestyle care.
• Who's here: Optical Express (£146.5M), Optegra (£99.8M), SpaMedica, Newmedica.

BLUE OCEAN · Consultant-Led Boutique

• Named consultant surgeon from initial scan to discharge.
• All 7–8 eye subspecialties under one roof, one trusted brand.
• 30–45 min unhurried visits with personal bedside manner.
• In-house ultra-clean theatre + dedicated WaveLight laser suite.
• EyeSpa dry eye clinic as a standalone wellness concept.
• Recurring subscriptions: LenzClub & SightPlan (~£134K Y3).
• Who's here: Nobody in the East Midlands. This is EyePros' category to own.

How Patients Arrive & Pay

HOW PATIENTS ARRIVE (ACQUISITION)

~25% Optometry & GP Referrals (Synergy Network):
Formal shared-care agreements across 450 regional practices. 1-click Syndesis digital referral portal. Optometrist post-op review fee (Estimated £45–£60).

~60% Direct to Patient (Digital Marketing & DTC):
Targeted Google PPC, SEO ranking for local conditions, patient video testimonials, and transparent online fixed pricing.

~15% Surgeon Access & Corporate Partnerships:
Independent visiting consultant surgeons bringing private lists; corporate wellness screening schemes.

WHO PAYS (REVENUE REALIZATION)

Direct card payment, bank transfer, and 0% APR patient financing over 10–36 months (£99–£160/month).

The high-converting optometrist referral engine powering 60% of revenue

The 7-Step Shared-Care Referral Pathway

1. Optometrist Identifies Patient: Practice detects cataract, glaucoma, dry eye or refractive need.
2. 1-Click Digital Referral: Optom refers via Syndesis portal (GOS18 & DICOM integration).
3. Rapid Acknowledgement: EyePros clinical coordination team acknowledges within 24 hours.
4. Named Consultant Surgery: Patient sees their named consultant at Kingfisher House.
5. 48-Hour Discharge Report: Comprehensive surgical summary transmitted back to optom.
6. 4-Week Post-Op Shared Care Review: Optometrist performs check and receives £45–£60 fee.
7. Patient Returned for Lifelong Care: Optometrist retains annual sight test & spectacle sales.
