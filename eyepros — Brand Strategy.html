Section 6: Recommended Brand Actions

Website Redesign

Pivot from clinical treatment focus to patient outcomes and lifestyle benefits. Introduce new visual identity and clear booking pathways.

Patient Story Video Series

Capture real patient journeys to humanize the brand and build trust through authentic testimonials.

Referrer Brand Pack

Develop premium informational materials for optometrists to reinforce the expert, subspecialty positioning.

Social Media Strategy

Shift to educational, myth-busting content, and behind-the-scenes clinic life to build community engagement.

Community Engagement

Host local eye health seminars and open days at Kingfisher House to build local brand awareness.

PR & Thought Leadership

Position Mr Ahmad Elsahn as a regional expert in MIGS and premium cataracts through trade and local media.
