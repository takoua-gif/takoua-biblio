Self-Pay Acceleration

Patients are increasingly unwilling to accept a 16–20 week NHS delay for basic monofocal lenses when private self-pay offers immediate treatment and premium lens options.

Premium IOL Demand

Surging consumer demand for EDOF and Trifocal lenses to achieve spectacle independence. NHS exclusively funds standard monofocal lenses, creating a natural upgrade pathway.

NHS Tariff Erosion

High-volume NHS tariff models (SpaMedica, CHEC) face margin pressure from NHS tariff cuts. EyePros's premium self-pay model is immune to tariff restructuring.
