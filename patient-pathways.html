Archetype 1 · Volume Driver

Cataract & Premium IOL Surgery

The highest-volume private ophthalmology service, converting NHS-delayed patients into rapid self-pay surgery with premium lens upgrades. Monofocal, Toric, and EDOF IOLs for freedom from glasses.

Procedure: 20–30 min per eye
High conversion rate
Bilateral or unilateral

Optometrist Referral

Optometrist identifies lens opacity. Refers via eReferral or direct EyePros pathway.

02
Biometry & Consultation

Precise IOL power calculation. Ahmad discusses lens options: Monofocal, Toric or EDOF.

03
Day Case Surgery

Local anaesthetic phacoemulsification. 20–30 minutes. Patient walks out same day.

04
Post-op Optometrist

Referring optometrist handles 1-week and 1-month reviews. Strengthens relationship cycle.

Key Differentiator

EyePros offers EDOF lenses (Tecnis Symfony, Clareon PanOptix) rarely available on NHS. Premium lens upgrades represent the core margin opportunity at £500–£1,200 above standard IOL pricing.

Archetype 2 · Specialist Niche

Glaucoma & MIGS Micro-Stent

Ahmad Elsahn's clinical specialty. Micro-Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS) offers a less invasive alternative to trabeculectomy with faster recovery. Reduces lifelong drop burden and IOP.

Ahmad's Primary Specialty
Lifetime subscription model
Combined with cataract
from £3,500 / eye
01
Optom Suspects Glaucoma

Elevated IOP, cupped disc, or visual field loss triggers urgent referral.

02
Specialist Assessment

OCT optic nerve, Humphrey visual fields, gonioscopy. Ahmad determines surgical candidacy.

03
MIGS Stent Implantation

iStent inject W or Hydrus microstent placed via corneal incision. Often combined with cataract extraction.

04
Long-term Monitoring

Annual IOP checks and visual field monitoring. Glaucoma subscription plan (£45/month) ties optometrists in as partners.

Subscription Opportunity

Glaucoma's chronic nature makes it ideal for annual monitoring packages. At £45/month, a patient enrolled for 10 years generates £5,400 in subscription revenue — dwarfing the initial surgical fee.

Medical-grade dry eye treatment addressing meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD), evaporative dry eye, and digital eye strain. The EyeSpa package combines IPL, LipiFlow, and BlephEx therapies.

High repeat attendance
Optometrist anchor service
Wide demographic appeal
from £350 / session

Archetype 5 · Innovation Pioneer

Valeda Photobiomodulation (Dry AMD)

EyePros is among a small number of UK centres offering Valeda® light therapy — the first CE-marked photobiomodulation device for dry Age-related Macular Degeneration. Addresses an unmet clinical need with no pharmacological alternative.

Unique regional offer
No NHS equivalent
High patient motivation
from £1,200 / course
01
OCT Confirmation

Drusen mapping, retinal thickness measurement. Confirms dry AMD (non-exudative) diagnosis.

02
Eligibility Check

Intermediate dry AMD confirmed. No wet AMD in either eye. Patient counselled on evidence level.

03
Valeda Treatment Course

9 sessions over 3 weeks. Non-invasive 3-minute light exposure. No discomfort or side effects.

04
Annual Retreatment

Clinical data supports annual re-courses. Long-term patient relationship with highest CLV in portfolio.

Strategic Note

Valeda is a first-mover differentiator in the Midlands. With 700k+ people living with AMD in the UK and NHS offering no disease-modifying treatment, this pathway commands premium pricing and exceptional patient loyalty.
