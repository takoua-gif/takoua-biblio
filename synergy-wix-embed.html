THE THREE QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

Every referral trusts someone else with a relationship you built.

You see this patient again long after surgery. How they are treated in between reflects on you. Synergy puts all three commitments in writing in our shared care agreement.

Question 1

Will I know what happened?

In Writing Structured discharge letter within 48 hours

Your letter arrives via Syndesis with procedure details, intraoperative findings, target refraction, postoperative medications, and specific guidance on what to monitor.

Question 2

Can I reach the surgeon if I am worried?

In Writing Direct consultant access, not a switchboard

Every case has a named escalation contact, plus direct access through our Urgent Clinical Helpline on 0333 77 272 77 (Option 5).

Question 3

Will my patient come back to me?

In Writing Protected 4-week review & clinical fee

Patients return to your practice for their 4-week postoperative review, and you receive an un-tiered shared care fee (up to £250 per eye) for your clinical chair time.

HOW A PATIENT MOVES

Defined responsibilities. Clear clinical timelines.

The exact 5-step shared care pathway agreed between your practice and EyePros.

1
Your Practice

Assess

Assess the patient, record clinical findings, and discuss standard and premium lens options so they make an informed choice.

2
Your Practice

Refer

Send referral through Syndesis. GOS 18, practice software, or bespoke secure messaging also work where criteria are met.

3
EyePros

Consent & Treat

Operating consultant personally leads consent and performs surgery, maintaining full clinical records and national audit submissions.

4
EyePros

Report Back

Receive discharge letter within 48 hours. Patient leaves theatre with written instructions and an emergency helpline number.

5
Your Practice

Follow Up

Carry out 4-week assessment and submit findings on Syndesis within 5 working days. Your shared care fee follows completed submission.

Clinical Helpline Active Throughout: From referral to final discharge, reach us via your named contact or Urgent Helpline.

Call Helpline (Opt 5)

Call us straight away if you find:

  • Unexplained reduction in VA
  • Significant corneal oedema
  • IOP of 30 mmHg or above
  • Choroidal haemorrhage
  • Signs of endophthalmitis
  • Wound dehiscence
Responsibility split: EyePros manages surgical complications, including further surgery. You conduct the post-op reviews and consult us before managing findings outside expected course.
