PARTNERSHIP VALUE

Why independent practices partner with EyePros

A collaborative framework designed to protect clinical relationships and reward post-operative care.

Continuity of Care for Your Patients

Following surgery, your patient returns to your practice for their 4-week postoperative review under agreed protocols.

Consultant Escalation & 24/7 Helpline

Named escalation contact for each patient, plus access to our Urgent Helpline on 0333 77 272 77 (Option 5).

Structured Reporting Within 48 Hours

EyePros issues a structured discharge letter via the Syndesis portal within 48 hours of surgery detailing findings and treatment.

Transparent Remuneration Without Quotas

Your chair time and scans are recognized through a transparent shared-care fee structure (up to £250/eye) with no volume targets.

THE PATHWAY

Five simple stages from assessment to discharge

Clear responsibilities and agreed timelines at each step of patient care.

1 Your Practice

Pre-Referral Assessment

Assess candidate against NICE eligibility criteria and discuss surgical choices.

2 Your Practice

Secure Referral

Submit via Syndesis portal (or GOS 18 / PMS) with diagnostic scans up to 64MB.

3 EyePros Surgical

Consent & Surgery

Operating consultant ophthalmologist personally leads surgical consent and treatment.

4 EyePros Surgical

48h Discharge Letter

Structured discharge letter arrives via Syndesis within 48 hours of surgery.

5 Your Practice

4-Week Review & Fee

Conduct post-op review, submit findings via portal, and receive your shared-care fee.
