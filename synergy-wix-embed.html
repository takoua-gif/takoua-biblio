THE SHARED-CARE PATHWAY

Five defined stages from assessment to discharge

Every stage of patient care has clearly designated clinical responsibilities and agreed timeframes.

1 Your Practice

Pre-Referral Assessment

Identify suitable candidates against NICE criteria. Discuss surgical options and lens choices with the patient.

2 Your Practice

Secure Referral Submission

Submit via Syndesis (or GOS 18 / PMS). Upload relevant diagnostic findings and OCT scans (up to 64MB).

3 EyePros Surgical

Consent & Treatment

Operating consultant ophthalmologist personally leads surgical consent and performs the procedure.

4 EyePros Surgical

48h Discharge Letter

Structured discharge letter arrives via Syndesis within 48 hours with written instructions and urgent contacts.

5 Your Practice

4-Week Review & Fee

Conduct the 4-week post-op assessment. Submit findings via Syndesis within 5 working days to release your fee.

Urgent Escalation Available Throughout: Both you and your patient have direct access to your named escalation contact and the Urgent Clinical Helpline on 0333 77 272 77, Option 5.

GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY

Clearly defined clinical responsibilities

Agreement v2.2 establishes unequivocal divisions of clinical accountability, legal indemnity, and patient safety.

Responsibilities of EyePros

  • Primary responsibility for surgical consent led by operating consultant ophthalmologist.
  • Delivery of procedures by GMC-registered surgical team members with verified procedural credentials.
  • Issue structured discharge letters via the portal within 48 hours of surgical treatment.
  • Retain full clinical responsibility for managing surgical complications.

Responsibilities of the Optometrist

  • Patient identification and shared decision-making consistent with NICE guidance.
  • Conduct 4-week postoperative assessments covering VA, IOP, OCT, and checking recovery.
  • Submit assessment reports accurately through the online portal within 5 working days.
  • Immediate contact with EyePros upon identification of clinical red flags.

Immediate Clinical Escalation Triggers (Agreement Section 4.3)

The Partner must contact the EyePros urgent line immediately upon identification of any of the following:

  • Unexplained VA loss
  • IOP ≥ 30 mmHg
  • Endophthalmitis signs
  • Wound dehiscence
  • Corneal oedema
  • Choroidal haemorrhage

EyePros Urgent Clinical Helpline

Available to both the partner optometrist and the patient with a named escalation contact for each individual case.
