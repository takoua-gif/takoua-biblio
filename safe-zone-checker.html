Platform Safe Zone & Format Guide

📸 Instagram Reels Safe Zones

Instagram Reels render in full screen 9:16 (1080×1920 px). However, the top 220px are obscured by the Reels title/camera bar and the bottom 320px contain user captions, account handle, and audio track details. Always center your hook text.

👍 Facebook Reels Guidelines

Facebook Reels share the 1080×1920 format. The action buttons (Like, Comment, Share) occupy a 60px strip along the right side of the screen. Keep logos and interactive overlays away from the right edge.

💼 LinkedIn Video Recommendations

LinkedIn supports both 9:16 vertical videos and 1:1 square / 16:9 landscape formats. On mobile, vertical videos feature player control bars and headline text across the bottom 240px.
