Platform Safe Zone & Format Guide

📸 Instagram Reels & Posts

Reels require 9:16 (1080×1920 px). Feed posts support 4:5 Portrait (1080×1350 px) or 1:1 Square. Safe zones ensure text stays clear of header titles and bottom captions.

👍 Facebook Reels & Feed

Reels use 9:16 vertical video. Keep logos clear of the right 50px side action buttons strip. Feed posts best utilize 1:1 square or 4:5 portrait formats.

💼 LinkedIn Posts & Videos

Supports 1:1 square graphics for posts, 16:9 landscape videos for long-form desktop playback, and 9:16 vertical videos for mobile feed discovery.

▶️ YouTube Shorts & Videos

Shorts render in 9:16 (1080×1920 px) with lower right subscribe buttons and title bar. Standard videos render in 16:9 landscape (1920×1080 px).
