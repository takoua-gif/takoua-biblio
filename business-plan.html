Brand Strategy

Brand Architecture & Clinical Leadership

Vision

Redefining Private Eye Care

To become the East Midlands' premier consultant-led ophthalmic hospital, seamlessly bridging advanced surgical technology with personalized patient continuity.

Mission

Rapid Access & Clinical Excellence

To eliminate anxiety and prolonged hospital wait times by providing rapid consultation, micro-incisional surgery, and bespoke vision restoration.

Brand Promise

"Specialist Care for Today, Long-Term Health for Future"

Every patient sees a named consultant surgeon from initial biometry scan through to post-operative discharge.

8 Strategic Priorities &
Market Opportunities

01 · Priority
Optometrist Partnerships

470 territory practices are the primary engine. Shared care fees and accredited CPD build an unshakeable moat.

02 · Priority
Private Consultations

Capturing self-pay cataract conversions from patients tired of 26-week NHS hospital eye service queues.

03 · Priority
Dry Eye / EyeSpa

20–30% adult prevalence. High-margin wellness packages and recurring maintenance visits with near-zero competition.

04 · Priority
Glaucoma Subscriptions

Lifetime management condition. 6-month IOP reviews and visual field tracking build recurring subscription revenue.

05 · Priority
Premium Diagnostics

IOLMaster 700 & Spectralis OCT establish diagnostic authority over high-street optical retailers.

06 · Priority
NHS Patient Contracts

Selective AQP slots with Nottingham ICB to balance theatre capacity while maintaining brand equity.

07 · Priority
Valeda for Dry AMD

First-mover photobiomodulation therapy addressing the previously untreated intermediate dry macular degeneration cohort.

08 · Priority
Compliance & Marketing

Strict adherence to ASA healthcare guidelines, GMC ethical codes, and CQC Single Assessment Framework.
